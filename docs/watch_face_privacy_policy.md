# Privacy Policy for Wear OS Watch Faces

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all Wear OS watch face applications ("Applications", "Watch Faces") developed and published by **Arutek** ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information handling across all of our watch faces published on the Google Play Store.

### 1. Zero Data Collection

Our watch face applications **do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or share any personal information, identifiable data, or usage metrics**.

Specifically:

* We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.

* We do not track location data (GPS or network-based).

* We do not run third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or telemetry tools.

* We do not transmit any data over the internet to external servers or third parties.

### 2. On-Device Sensor and System Information

To function as an interactive watch face, our applications may read and display system data provided by the Wear OS platform on your paired smartwatch. This information is processed **entirely locally on your device in real time** and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted off the device.

Depending on the specific watch face and features enabled, local data may include:

* **Time and Date:** Synchronized with your device clock.

* **Battery Status:** Percentage and charging status displayed on watch complications or indicators.

* **Step Count and Fitness Metrics:** Daily step counts or activity data gathered via standard system health sensors or the system step counter service.

* **User Customization Preferences:** Display color themes, complication slots, and visual toggles selected via on-device customization menus. These settings are stored locally on your device using Wear OS platform storage.

### 3. Permissions

Our watch faces only request or utilize permissions strictly necessary to display on-screen elements (such as `BODY_SENSORS` or `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` if step or heart-rate complications are active). These permissions are handled entirely by the Wear OS operating system. Granting these permissions does not transmit any of your biometric or sensor data to us or any third party.

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Our watch faces are suitable for general audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13. Because our applications do not collect or request any personal information from any user, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Family Policy requirements.

### 5. Third-Party Services

Our watch faces do not integrate third-party APIs, external ad networks, or data brokers. Distribution, installation, and purchases (if applicable) are handled exclusively through the Google Play Store and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our watch faces following any modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
